The Problem

Navigating a World Built for Sight

Visually impaired individuals face significant challenges navigating both indoor and outdoor environments. Traditional white canes only provide feedback after physical contact — which can lead to accidents and injuries.

Existing smart canes attempt to solve this, but often suffer from critical limitations that prevent widespread adoption:

  • ⏱
    Delayed obstacle detection — feedback arrives too late to react safely
  • 💸
    High cost makes devices inaccessible to most users globally
  • 📦
    Bulky and inconvenient designs reduce everyday adoption
  • 🔗
    Constant dependence on cane usage is restricting and inconvenient

The Solution

Introducing Vision Path

Vision Path is a compact, torch-like handheld device that provides real-time obstacle detection using ultrasonic sensing technology. Simple, effective, and accessible for all age groups.

Unlike traditional solutions, it requires no smartphone, no internet, and no continuous cane usage — just point, and navigate with complete confidence.

The Process

How It Works

Vision Path transforms ultrasonic signals into clear, intuitive feedback — in real time, every time.

01

Emit & Detect

The HC-SR04 sensor emits ultrasonic pulses and measures the time for the echo to return from nearby obstacles.

02

Calculate Distance

The Arduino Nano processes the echo duration to calculate precise distance, triggering alerts when an object enters the 50–100 cm safe zone.

03

Dual Alert

Both the buzzer and vibration motor activate simultaneously — ensuring clear feedback regardless of the surrounding environment.

04

Navigate Safely

With advance warning, the user steers clear of obstacles — moving confidently through any space, indoors or outdoors.
