The Challenge & Our Answer

Why Vision Path Exists

Millions of visually impaired individuals navigate a world not built for them. Vision Path changes that — with proactive intelligence, not reactive aid.

⚠️

The Problem

Visually impaired individuals face significant challenges navigating daily environments. Traditional white canes only detect obstacles after physical contact — putting users at constant risk.

The primary challenge is the significant difficulty visually impaired and blind individuals face while navigating daily environments. Traditional mobility aids such as standard white canes offer limited detection and frequently fail to identify hazards above the knee or sudden surface changes like potholes, stairs, and speed breakers in real time.

This lack of comprehensive environmental awareness results in serious safety risks, increased mental stress, and heavy dependence on external assistance — ultimately reducing the user's confidence and social independence.

✨

The Solution

Vision Path introduces intelligent ultrasonic sensing in a compact, handheld device — delivering continuous environmental awareness through haptic, audio, and visual feedback simultaneously.

Vision Path introduces a compact and intelligent assistive device that utilises advanced ultrasonic sensing technology to provide continuous environmental monitoring. Unlike traditional tools, this system offers immediate awareness through a multimodal feedback mechanism:

● Haptic vibrations for silent, discreet alerts
● Audible buzzer alerts in noisy environments
● Visual LED indications for low-light conditions

Integrated into a lightweight, energy-efficient handheld form factor, the device lets users detect walls, stairs, and uneven pathways instantly — ensuring safer, more fluid navigation indoors and outdoors.

The Device

Introducing Vision Path

Vision Path is a compact, torch-like handheld device that provides real-time obstacle detection using ultrasonic sensing. Simple, effective, and accessible for all age groups.

Unlike traditional solutions, it requires no smartphone, no internet, and no continuous cane usage — just point, and navigate with complete confidence.

The Process

How It Works

Vision Path transforms ultrasonic signals into clear, intuitive feedback — in real time, every time.

01

Emit & Detect

The HC-SR04 sensor emits ultrasonic pulses and measures the time for the echo to return from nearby obstacles.

02

Calculate Distance

The Arduino Nano processes echo duration to calculate precise distance, triggering alerts when an object enters the 50–100 cm zone.

03

Dual Alert

Both the buzzer and vibration motor activate simultaneously — ensuring clear feedback regardless of the surrounding environment.

04

Navigate Safely

With advance warning, the user steers clear of obstacles — moving confidently through any space, indoors or outdoors.
