Key Findings

Trial Observations

Summary of key findings and performance notes gathered across all three trial sessions.

⚡

Consistent Alert Timing

Obstacle alerts fired reliably within the 50–100 cm detection window across all environments, giving users adequate reaction time.

🔔

Dual Feedback Effective

The buzzer + vibration combination proved useful — vibration helped in noisy outdoor settings where audio feedback was harder to hear.

💡

LED Assist Valuable

Night trial confirmed that the built-in LED meaningfully improves user confidence in low-visibility environments.

🎯

Zero False Negatives

All solid obstacles within detection range triggered alerts in every trial — no missed detections were recorded across the three sessions.
