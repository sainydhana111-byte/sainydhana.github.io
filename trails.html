Key Findings

Trial Observations

Summary of key findings gathered across all three sessions at Netra Vidyalaya, Bheemili — covering both indoor classroom environments and outdoor open grounds.

⚡

Consistent Alert Timing

Obstacle alerts fired reliably within the 50–100 cm detection window across all indoor and outdoor scenarios, giving users adequate reaction time in every trial.

🔔

Dual Feedback Effective

The buzzer + vibration combination proved effective in the classroom — vibration was especially useful in quieter indoor spaces where audio seemed intrusive.

🏫

Classroom-Ready

Dense desk and chair arrangements at Netra Vidyalaya created an ideal test bed — Vision Path navigated them with zero missed detections in both indoor trials.

🛒

Immediate Purchase Interest

A school student who tested the device outdoors requested to purchase it on the spot — a direct, unscripted signal of real-world value and user satisfaction.
